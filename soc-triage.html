02

Behind the workflow

Designed for
decisive analysis.

01

Prioritize what matters

Severity alone is not a decision. The queue pairs severity with confidence, asset context, and potential impact so the analyst can start with the event most likely to matter.

02

Keep evidence close

Core entities, MITRE technique mapping, and source details stay beside the alert. The analyst gets enough context to form a hypothesis without losing their place in the queue.

03

Make state visible

New, investigating, and closed states clarify ownership and reduce duplicate work. In a production version, every transition would be timestamped and added to an immutable case history.

04

Next iteration

Planned extensions include ingestion from sample Splunk events, analyst notes, enrichment checks, an escalation path, and a documented incident timeline.
