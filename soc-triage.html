Behind the workflow

Designed for
decisive analysis.

01

Normalize the signal

The intake layer accepts plain text, syslog, JSON, CSV, key-value, CEF, and LEEF records, then converts the selected alert into a consistent form for analysis.

02

Make the score explainable

Every risk point is tied to a named rule and visible evidence. Analysts can challenge the result instead of accepting an opaque severity label.

03

Connect findings to action

Validated indicators and supported evidence map to specific MITRE ATT&CK techniques, defensive recommendations, and a clear escalation disposition.

04

Preserve the investigation

Sanitized results can be correlated with local history and exported as Markdown, JSON, or PDF incident reports. The app recommends response steps but never executes them.
